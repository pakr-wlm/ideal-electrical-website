Family owned for over 60 years

Ideal Electrical Contractors was founded in May 1959 by brothers Bobby and Ray Lindsey. The company grew over the years as they added commercial and industrial work to their residential jobs.

Ray retired in 1992, and Mike Lindsey bought his stake. Bobby retired in 2019 after 60 years. Today Mike, Bobby's youngest son, owns and runs the company.
